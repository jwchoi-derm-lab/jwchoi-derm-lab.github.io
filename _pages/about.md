## 🔬 Core Research Pillars

<div class="pillars-grid">
  
  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-icon">🧬</div>
    <div class="pillar-content">
      <h3>Skin Microbiome & Shotgun Metagenomics</h3>
      <p>Investigating taxonomic, functional, and strain-level dysbiosis in skin malignancies and inflammatory dermatoses using reproducible bioinformatic pipelines and metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs).</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-icon">🩺</div>
    <div class="pillar-content">
      <h3>Dermato-Oncology & Dermato-Surgery</h3>
      <p>Optimizing clinical outcomes, surgical margins, and reconstructive flap designs for melanoma and non-melanoma skin cancer (NMSC) with nationwide clinical registries.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-icon">📊</div>
    <div class="pillar-content">
      <h3>Pharmacoepidemiology & Health Big Data</h3>
      <p>Conducting survival modeling, propensity score matching, and interrupted time series (ITS) analyses utilizing nationwide health claims data (HIRA/NHIS).</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-icon">💇</div>
    <div class="pillar-content">
      <h3>Hair & Nail Unit Pathology</h3>
      <p>Translational research and clinical trials on alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, and innovative surgical reconstruction of nail apparatus dystrophies.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

</div>
