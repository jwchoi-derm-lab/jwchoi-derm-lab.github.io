## 🎯 Purpose of this Study

While population aging and ultraviolet (UV) radiation drive rising skin cancer incidence, growing evidence indicates that the tumor microenvironment harbors complex microbial communities that modulate local immune surveillance, T-cell infiltration, and carcinogenesis. Crucially, a substantial fraction of cutaneous microorganisms remain uncultured, uncharacterized, and absent from conventional reference databases—collectively termed **Microbial Dark Matter (MDM)**.

This study aims to:
- Identify and catalog unmapped MDM (novel bacteria, fungi, and viruses) in skin cancer by constructing de novo **Metagenome-Assembled Genomes (MAGs)** from paired tissue and non-invasive samples.
- Discover and quantify tumor-specific **microbe-derived proteins and oncomicrobial peptides** using high-resolution LC-MS/MS proteogenomics.
- Elucidate host-microbiome immunomodulatory mechanisms influencing tumor recurrence, T-cell exhaustion, and therapeutic resistance in Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC), Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC), Actinic Keratosis (AK), and Cutaneous Melanoma.

## 🔬 Overview & Rationale

Conventional microbiome studies rely solely on reference-based 16S amplicon profiling, missing uncultivated microorganisms and their functional protein products.

## 🛠️ Methodological Features

<div class="method-grid">

    <h4>Multi-Tier Spatial Sampling Design</h4>
    <p>Integration of paired invasive tissue biopsies with non-invasive surface tape stripping and swabs across premalignant and malignant skin lesions.</p>

    <p>Sensitive quantitative proteomics capturing low-abundance microbial peptides within the complex host tumor microenvironment.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="method-card">
    <div class="method-icon">🤝</div>

    <p>Computational correlation of microbial protein expression with host T-cell infiltration, local cytokines, and recurrence risk profiles.</p>

## 💡 Translational & Clinical Significance

1. **Methodological Landmark in Solid Tumors**: Establishes the first systematic proteogenomic framework for unmapped Microbial Dark Matter in cutaneous oncology.
2. **Next-Generation Non-Invasive Biotherapeutics**: Discovers targetable microbial proteins and bioactive metabolites as candidates for topical biotherapeutics (reducing topical chemotherapy irritation).
3. **Early Biomarkers for High-Risk Populations**: Builds an integrated multi-omics database to facilitate non-invasive screening tools for immunocompromised organ transplant recipients and elderly patients.
